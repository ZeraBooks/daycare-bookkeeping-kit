# Month-end checklist: daycare or child care center

Work top to bottom. Money comes from three directions (parents, the state
subsidy program and the CACFP food program), and each pays on its own
schedule, so each gets its own step.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Bank and card statements for the full month, as PDFs
- [ ] Payments report from your child care software, by family
- [ ] Subsidy remittance notices for every subsidy deposit
- [ ] CACFP payment notices
- [ ] Payroll report for every pay run in the month

## 2. Parent tuition
- [ ] Tuition recorded by family, not just as a daily total
- [ ] Registration, late pickup and activity fees in their own account
- [ ] Tuition paid ahead for next month held as prepaid tuition
- [ ] Families behind on tuition listed

## 3. Subsidy
- [ ] Each subsidy deposit matched to its remittance notice
- [ ] Remittance split by child and weeks covered
- [ ] Each family's parent copay compared with what the subsidy did not
      cover
- [ ] Attendance submitted but not yet paid listed, so you know what is
      still coming

## 4. Food program
- [ ] CACFP deposit booked as food program income, noting which month's meals
      it pays for
- [ ] Food cost compared with food income over the quarter, not the month

## 5. Payroll from the report
- [ ] Gross wages, employer taxes and withholdings booked from the payroll
      report, not the net pay leaving the bank

## 6. Other costs
- [ ] Food, classroom supplies, rent, insurance and fees booked to their own
      accounts
- [ ] Home daycare: household bills kept and the hours log up to date for the
      time-space calculation

## 7. Reconcile and review
- [ ] Every bank and card account ties to its printed closing balance
- [ ] Revenue by source compared with last month
- [ ] Uncategorized at zero

## 8. Lock and send
- [ ] Month locked so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Reports sent to the owner or director, with open questions listed

## In January
- [ ] Payments by family totaled for the year (`family_year_end.py`)
- [ ] Year-end statements sent to parents with your name, address and
      taxpayer ID
